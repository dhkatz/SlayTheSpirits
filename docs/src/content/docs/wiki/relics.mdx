import RelicList from '../../../components/RelicList.astro';

Tavern relics never drop from chests, elites or shops. The only way to get one is to take a **Shot** at a
[rest site](../../play/rest-sites/), which offers 3 you don't have yet.

"Drink" here means any drink except Water.
