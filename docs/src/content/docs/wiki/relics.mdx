Tavern relics never drop from chests, elites or shops. The only way to get one is to take a **Shot** at a
[rest site](../../play/rest-sites/), which offers 3 you don't have yet. "Drink" here means any drink except Water.

**Tavern runes** are Hextech runes for the [ARAM: Mayhem](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3747501308)
mod. With it installed, its rune choice at the start of each act can offer them in multiplayer runs, next to its own
runes (Silver, Gold or Prismatic, like the act's choice). Character runes are only offered to that character. The host
can switch them off under **Settings → Spirits → Tavern Runes**, and each one can be switched off in ARAM's own
config menu, where they're grouped under "Slay the Spirits".
