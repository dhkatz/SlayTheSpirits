Tavern events turn up in "?" rooms in any act of a multiplayer run, while the **Tavern Events** setting is on (and the
drinking game is). Each player gets their own copy and makes their own choice. Drinks you take at an event are your
choice, so the per-act limits never [step them down](../../play/how-it-works/#caps).
