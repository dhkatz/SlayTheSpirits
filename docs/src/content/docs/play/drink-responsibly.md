---
title: Drink responsibly
description: Nobody has to drink, and nobody should drive.
---

Slay the Spirits is a party game for adults who choose to drink. Some ground rules for the table:

- **Nobody has to drink.** Anyone can play with water or soda. The **Sober** rule set renames every drink (soda
  instead of beer, pickle juice instead of liquor), and those drinks still count for Tipsy and the cards that use it.
- **Know your limit.** A Shot is a full standard drink, and a Chug is a whole can or bottle. Lower the per-act caps
  or pick **Casual** for a lighter night. Rest site drinks and Tavern potions are always optional.
- **Drink water.** By default, every rest site hands out a Water. Take it.
- **Never drink and drive.** Sort out rides home before the first boss.
- **Follow the law** on drinking age where you are.

If someone at the table has had enough, they can stop. Hand them the *Designated Driver*.
