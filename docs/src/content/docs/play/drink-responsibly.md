---
title: Drink Responsibly
description: Nobody has to drink, and nobody should drive.
---

Slay the Spirits is a party game for adults who choose to drink. Some ground rules for the table:

- **Nobody has to drink.** Anyone can play with water or soda. The [Sober rule set](../rule-sets/) renames every
  drink (soda instead of beer, pickle juice instead of liquor), and those drinks still count for :kw[Tipsy] and the
  cards that use it.
- **Know your limit.** A :kw[Shot] is a full standard drink, and a :kw[Chug] is a whole can or bottle. Lower the
  [per-act caps](../how-it-works/#caps) or pick [Casual](../rule-sets/) for a lighter night.
  [Rest site drinks](../rest-sites/) and [Tavern potions](../../wiki/potions/) are always optional.
- **Drink water.** By default, every rest site hands out a :kw[Water]. Take it.
- **Never drink and drive.** Sort out rides home before the first boss.
- **Follow the law** on drinking age where you are.

If someone at the table has had enough, they can stop. Hand them the :card[Designated Driver].
