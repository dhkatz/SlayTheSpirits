The host picks a rule set under **Settings → Spirits → Rule Set**. Change any rule and it becomes **Custom**. Your
last custom rules are remembered next to the built-in ones.

## Built-in rule sets

- **Casual**: Sips and the odd finished drink. Never a Chug or a Shot.
- **Standard**: the default. Everyone Chugs when a boss shows up, and a Shot only when the run is lost.
- **Degenerate**: elites and curses empty glasses, dying costs a Shot, and the run ends with a Shot either way.
- **Sober**: Standard, with soda instead of beer and pickle juice instead of liquor.

"That Player" means whoever the moment is about: who died, who got hit, who drew the curse.

## Settings

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| Drinking Game On | Master switch. When off, no drinks are handed out, but Tavern content can still appear. |
| Confirm Each Drink | A player handed a drink by someone else's card accepts it before it plays. |
| Tavern Cards | Colorless Tavern cards appear in rewards and shops. |
| Character Cards | Each character's own Tavern card appears in their rewards. |
| Tavern Potions | Light Beer, Tallboy and Firewater drop like other potions. |
| Rest Site Drinks | Off, Once per Act or Every Rest Site. See [Rest sites](../rest-sites/). |
| Who Drinks When | For each moment: on or off, who drinks, how many, and which drink. |

Per-act caps, Tipsy amounts, drink names and per-enemy rules aren't in the Settings tab yet. They can be edited in
`profile.json`, in the mod's storage folder.

## Sharing rules

**Copy Share Code** puts your rule set on the clipboard as a short code. Anyone can load it with **Paste Share Code**
to play by the same house rules.
