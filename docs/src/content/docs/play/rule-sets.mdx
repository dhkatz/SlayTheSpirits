The host picks a rule set under **Settings → Spirits → Rule Set**, and everyone plays by it for the whole run. Change
any rule and it becomes **Custom**. Your last custom rules are remembered next to the built-in ones.

- **That Player** is whoever the moment is about: who died, who got hit, who drew the curse.

## Settings

Everything on the **Spirits** settings tab. Only the host's settings count.

| Setting | What It Does |
|---|---|
| **Drinking Game On** | The master switch. When off, no drinks are handed out, but Tavern content can still appear. |
| **Confirm Each Drink** | A player handed a drink by someone else's card accepts it before it plays. |
| **Tavern Cards** | The [colorless Tavern cards](../../wiki/cards/?pool=colorless) appear in rewards and shops. |
| **Character Cards** | Each character's own [Tavern cards](../../wiki/cards/) appear in their rewards. |
| **Tavern Potions** | :potion[Light Beer], :potion[Tallboy] and :potion[Firewater] drop like other potions. |
| **Tavern Events** | The [Speakeasy and Seven Grand](../events/) can turn up in "?" rooms. |
| **Rest Site Tabs** | Off, Once per Act or Every Rest Site. See [Rest Sites](../rest-sites/). |
| **Who Drinks When** | For each moment in the table above: on or off, who drinks, how many, and which drink. |

Per-act limits, :kw[Tipsy] amounts, drink names and per-enemy rules aren't on the settings tab yet. They can be
edited in `profile.json`, in the mod's storage folder.

## Sharing Rules

**Copy Share Code** puts your rule set on the clipboard as a short code. Anyone can load it with **Paste Share Code**
to play by the same house rules.
