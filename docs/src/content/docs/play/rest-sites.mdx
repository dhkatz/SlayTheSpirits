import Panel from '../../../components/Panel.astro';
import PanelGrid from '../../../components/PanelGrid.astro';
import { rules } from '../../../lib/data';

export const ruleSetsWith = (limit) =>
	rules.presets
		.filter((preset) => preset.restSiteDrinks === limit)
		.map((preset) => preset.name)
		.join(', ') || 'None';

Next to Rest and Smith, rest sites offer two drinks you can choose to take. Taking one uses your action at that rest
site, like Rest or Smith.

<PanelGrid>
<Panel title="Chug" image="generated/images/rest_site/spirits_chug.png">

**The drink:** :kw[Chug] a whole drink.

**The reward:** choose 2 of 5 [Tavern cards](../../wiki/cards/) to add to your deck, from the
[colorless set](../../wiki/cards/?pool=colorless) plus your character's own card.

</Panel>
<Panel title="Take a Shot" image="generated/images/rest_site/spirits_shot.png">

**The drink:** take a :kw[Shot] of liquor.

**The reward:** choose 1 of 3 [Tavern relics](../../wiki/relics/) you don't have yet. They can't be found anywhere
else. Once you own them all, you get a normal relic instead.

</Panel>
</PanelGrid>

The reward comes first. If you back out of the card or relic choice, you don't drink and can still Rest or Smith.
Backing out and choosing again shows the same cards or relics, so there's no rerolling.

These drinks are your choice, so they are never [stepped down](../how-it-works/#caps) by the per-act limits. Separately,
the built-in rule sets hand everyone a :kw[Water] at every rest site.

## How Often

The **Rest Site Drinks** setting decides how often each player can take them.

| Rest Site Drinks | What It Means | Rule Sets |
|---|---|---|
| **Off** | Rest sites only have the game's own options. | {ruleSetsWith('Off')} |
| **Once per Act** | Each player can Chug once and take a Shot once per act. Drinks from other sources, like dying, don't use these up. | {ruleSetsWith('OncePerAct')} |
| **Every Rest Site** | No limit. | {ruleSetsWith('Unlimited')} |

See [Rule Sets](../rule-sets/) for everything else each rule set changes.
