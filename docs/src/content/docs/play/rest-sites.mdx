import Panel from '../../../components/Panel.astro';
import PanelGrid from '../../../components/PanelGrid.astro';
import { rules } from '../../../lib/data';

export const ruleSetsWith = (limit) =>
	rules.presets
		.filter((preset) => preset.restSiteTabs === limit)
		.map((preset) => preset.name)
		.join(', ') || 'None';

Next to Rest and Smith, rest sites let you put a reward on your tab. Taking one uses your action at that rest site,
like Rest or Smith. You don't drink now: you pay later, with a Tavern curse in your deck that makes you drink and gets
in your way.

<PanelGrid>
<Panel title="Open a Tab" image="generated/images/rest_site/spirits_chug.png">

**The reward:** choose 2 of 5 [Tavern cards](../../wiki/cards/) to add to your deck, from the
[colorless set](../../wiki/cards/?pool=colorless) plus your character's own cards.

**The tab:** a random minor curse: :card[Spill], :card[Drunk Text], :card[Hiccups], :card[Bar Fly] or
:card[Sticky Floor].

</Panel>
<Panel title="Run Up the Tab" image="generated/images/rest_site/spirits_shot.png">

**The reward:** choose 1 of 3 [Tavern relics](../../wiki/relics/) you don't have yet. Once you own them all, you
get a normal relic instead.

**The tab:** a random major curse: :card[Hangover], :card[Beer Goggles] or :card[Fuzzy Memory]. These come from
nowhere else.

</Panel>
</PanelGrid>

The reward comes first. If you back out of the card or relic choice, you get no curse and can still Rest or Smith.
Backing out and choosing again shows the same cards or relics, so there's no rerolling. The curse is a surprise until
it lands in your deck, and in the built-in rule sets gaining a curse is a :kw[Sip] on the spot.

The Tavern's **Settle Up** pays off a tab: :kw[Finish Your Drink]{label="finish your drink"} to remove one Tavern
curse from your deck. Card removal at a shop works too.

## How Often

The **Rest Site Tabs** setting decides how often each player can take them.

| Rest Site Tabs | What It Means | Rule Sets |
|---|---|---|
| **Off** | Rest sites only have the game's own options. | {ruleSetsWith('Off')} |
| **Once per Act** | Each player can open a tab once and run one up once per act. | {ruleSetsWith('OncePerAct')} |
| **Every Rest Site** | No limit. | {ruleSetsWith('Unlimited')} |

Separately, the built-in rule sets hand everyone a :kw[Water] at every rest site.

See [Rule Sets](../rule-sets/) for everything else each rule set changes.
