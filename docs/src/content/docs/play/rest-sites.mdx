Next to Rest and Smith, rest sites offer two drinks you can choose to take. Taking one uses your action at that rest
site, like Rest or Smith.

| Option | The drink | The reward |
|---|---|---|
| **Chug** | Chug a whole drink. | Choose 2 of 5 Tavern cards (the colorless set plus your character's own) to add to your deck. |
| **Take a Shot** | Take a shot of liquor. | Choose 1 of 3 [Tavern relics](../../wiki/relics/) you don't have yet. They can't be found anywhere else. Once you own them all, you get a normal relic instead. |

The reward comes first. If you back out of the card or relic choice, you don't drink and can still Rest or Smith.
Backing out and choosing again shows the same cards or relics, so there's no rerolling.

## How often

The **Rest Site Drinks** setting decides:

- **Off**: rest sites only have the game's own options.
- **Once per Act**: each player can Chug once and take a Shot once per act. Drinks from other sources, like dying,
  don't use these up.
- **Every Rest Site**: no limit.
