Game moments, such as a boss fight starting, a player dying or a big hit, hand out drinks according to the host's
[rule set](../rule-sets/). Tavern [cards](../../wiki/cards/), [relics](../../wiki/relics/) and
[potions](../../wiki/potions/) hand out more. The game announces each one, and your job is to actually drink it.

## The Drinks

From lightest to heaviest. Everything below a :kw[Shot] is measured against the drink in your hand.

<Ladder />

How often each comes up in the Standard rule set:

- :kw[Sip]{label="Sips"} are common: most rules, every Tavern card and every [Tavern potion](../../wiki/potions/).
  Stronger cards cost more Sips.
- :kw[Finish Your Drink]{label="Finishing your drink"} is uncommon: dying, winning the run, and the Tavern.
- :kw[Chug]{label="Chugs"} and :kw[Shot]{label="Shots"} are rare: the boss-fight Chug, a Shot when the run is lost,
  and drinks you choose yourself at the Tavern.

No card in your deck makes you do more than Sip.

## Tipsy

Every drink adds to your :kw[Tipsy], a buff that shows how much you've had this run. It never goes down during a
run. Some Tavern cards and relics get stronger with it, such as :card[Down the Hatch], :card[Hair of the Dog] and
the :relic[Hip Flask]. Hover over a player's Tipsy to see their drinks so far.

## The Bar Tab

During combat, a counter for each kind of drink sits under each player, showing how many Sips, Finishes and so on
they've been handed this fight. Use it to check everyone has kept up. It resets every combat.

When a card makes someone else drink, the drink appears as a status card that plays itself. With **Confirm Each
Drink** on, the drinker accepts it first on a card-selection screen.

## Caps

Each [rule set](../rule-sets/#compare-the-rule-sets) can cap how many of each heavy drink a player takes per act. A
drink that would go over the cap is stepped down one rung at a time: a :kw[Shot] over the cap becomes a :kw[Chug],
and so on. In Casual, Chugs and Shots are capped at zero, so they never happen.

Drinks you **choose** ([rest site](../rest-sites/) drinks and [Tavern potions](../../wiki/potions/)) are never
stepped down, because that would change the price of what you picked. They still count toward the caps for drinks
you didn't choose.

## Skipping and Passing Drinks

- :card[Designated Driver] makes you immune to drinks for the rest of the combat. :kw[Water] still counts.
- :card[Slip Away] passes the next drink you'd get to a random teammate.
