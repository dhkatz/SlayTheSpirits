Two bars can turn up in "?" rooms in any act, in multiplayer runs while the **Tavern Events** setting is on (and the
drinking game is). Each player gets their own copy and makes their own choice. Drinks you take here are your choice,
so the per-act limits never [step them down](../how-it-works/#caps).

<PanelGrid>
<Panel title="Speakeasy" image="generated/images/events/spirits_speakeasy.png">

A door in an alley, a slot at eye level. *"Password?"*

- **"Swordfish."** :kw[Finish Your Drink]{label="Finish your drink"}. Choose 1 of 3 upgraded
  [Tavern cards](../../wiki/cards/).
- **Bluff.** Even odds: get in free and choose 1 of 3 Tavern cards, or get thrown out and lose 15% of your Max HP.
- **Leave.**

</Panel>
<Panel title="Seven Grand" image="generated/images/events/spirits_seven_grand.png">

A whiskey bar with a wall of bottles and pool tables in the back.

- **Bartender's pick.** Take a :kw[Shot]. Obtain a random Rare relic.
- **Whiskey flight.** Take 3 :kw[Sip]{label="Sips"}. Upgrade 2 cards.
- **Rack 'em up.** Bet 50 Gold on a game of pool. Win 100 Gold, or lose the bet and take a :kw[Sip]. Loser drinks.
- **Leave.**

</Panel>
</PanelGrid>
